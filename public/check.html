Step 2 · get told when this changes

AI-crawler policy is not static — domains flip between blocked and allowed every week. We scan the whole top 50,000 weekly. If anything changes for your domain, you get one email naming exactly what changed. Nothing else.

  1. If you're on Cloudflare (about 1 in 5 sites are): enable AI Crawl Control, join the Pay Per Crawl beta, and set your price — minimum $0.01, one price for all charged crawlers. Cloudflare collects and pays out; you never touch the plumbing.

  3. Everyone: declare machine-readable terms with RSL and robots.txt so your policy is visible to every compliant crawler.
